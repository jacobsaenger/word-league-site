Common questions

How do I join a league?
Ask someone in the league for its six-letter invite code, then tap Join with a code and enter it. Joining is always free.

Who pays for a league?
Only the host. Basic hosts 1 league with up to 6 players; Plus hosts up to 3 leagues with up to 12 players each. Both start with a 7-day free trial.

How do I cancel my plan?
On iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Word League. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

I got a new phone. How do I keep my results?
Join your league again with the invite code and the same name, then tap "That's me". To keep accounts safe, open Word League on your old phone and approve it there. If your old phone is gone, a league admin can approve it instead, as long as you don't have a plan and all your leagues are run by that admin. Otherwise, email us and we'll move your account over.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, open Settings → Delete my account. This removes your results and leaves all your leagues.
