Word League Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Word League is made by Nova Meridia LLC ("we"). It is a daily word-puzzle game you play in private leagues with family and friends. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • The name you choose. When you start, you pick a display name (up to 24 characters). There is no email address, phone number or password. Your account is an anonymous sign-in that lives on your device.
  • League information. The names of leagues you create or join, their invite codes, who is in each league, and who hosts it.
  • Game results. For each daily puzzle: whether you solved it, how many tries it took, how long it took, your guesses and your in-progress game state, so the scoreboard, streaks and replays work and you can continue on another device.
  • League votes. Requests and votes to re-roll or reset a puzzle, and league ownership transfers.
  • Subscription status. If you host a league with a Basic or Plus plan, we store the plan, the store's transaction reference, the renewal date and whether you are in a free trial. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (or Google Play). We never see your card details or your Apple ID.

What we don't collect

  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.
  • No advertising, advertising identifiers or tracking across other apps and websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.

The daily 9pm reminder is scheduled on your device. It does not send anything to us.

How it's used and who can see it

Everything above is used only to run the game. Your display name, results and votes are visible to the other members of the leagues you belong to. People outside your leagues can't see them. We don't sell or share your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase, which hosts Word League's database. Subscriptions are checked with Apple's App Store (or Google Play) when you buy or restore a plan. These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, results and league memberships are deleted. Leagues you created stay available to their remaining members.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete my account. This removes your data from our database.
  • Leave a league: Settings → Leave league.
  • Cancel a plan: manage or cancel it in your App Store subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Reminders: turn notifications off in your device's Settings.

Children

Word League is suitable for all ages and has no chat or public profiles. We don't knowingly collect more than the information described above from anyone, including children under 13. A parent who believes their child's data should be removed can delete the account in the app or contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
